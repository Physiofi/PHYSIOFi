1. Terms of Service

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Nature of Service

PhysioFi is a platform designed to facilitate communication between users and physiotherapy professionals. We provide an inquiry and booking request system; we do not provide medical advice or immediate clinical services through this website.

2. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

The use of this website or the submission of a contact form does not create a doctor-patient relationship. Such a relationship is only established after a formal consultation and agreement with the physiotherapy group.

3. Limitation of Liability

PhysioFi and its operators are not liable for any delays in communication, missed appointments, or the quality of care provided by the final practitioner. We act solely as a coordination tool.

4. Emergency Disclaimer

DO NOT USE THIS SITE FOR MEDICAL EMERGENCIES. If you are experiencing an emergency, please call your local emergency services immediately.

2. Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you use our booking request form, we collect:

Identity Data: Name.

Contact Data: Email address and phone number.

Health-Related Data: A brief description of your physical concern or "issue."

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used exclusively to:

Identify your specific needs.

Allow our team to contact you to schedule a session.

Maintain a record of your request.

3. Data Storage & Third Parties

As a frontend-focused site, we use third-party tools to process form submissions. By submitting the form, you acknowledge that your data may be processed by these providers in accordance with their security standards. We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to ensure your data is handled securely. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Users share their health-related concerns at their own discretion.
