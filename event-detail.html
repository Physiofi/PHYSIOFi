8 Days. 1 Mission. Better Recovery.
September 1–8, 2026
PHYSIOFi — Redefining Recovery

Join PHYSIOFi for eight days dedicated to movement, recovery, rehabilitation, physiotherapy awareness and community engagement.

From online educational sessions and interactive movement challenges to an offline physiotherapy camp and a World Physiotherapy Day celebration, PHYSIOFi Physiotherapy Week 2026 brings the message of better movement and recovery to the community.

ASSESS MOVE RECOVER RETURN CHALLENGE CONNECT CELEBRATE INSPIRE

More event highlights

DAY 1 — SEPTEMBER 1

ASSESS

Know Your Movement

The campaign begins by helping people understand why movement matters. Explore topics such as understanding common movement problems, the role of physiotherapy, pain and movement awareness, posture, everyday habits, and when professional assessment may be relevant.

DAY 2 — SEPTEMBER 2

MOVE

Move Better

Day 2 focuses on movement awareness, mobility and healthy lifestyle habits, helping people build better functional movement through posture, flexibility, exercise patterns and everyday activity.

DAY 3 — SEPTEMBER 3

RECOVER

Understanding Recovery

Recovery is a process—not simply the absence of pain. This day explores rehabilitation principles including injury recovery, post-operative rehab, musculoskeletal rehabilitation, exercise progression and functional recovery.

DAY 4 — SEPTEMBER 4

RETURN

Get Back to What You Love

Recovery becomes meaningful when it helps people return to the activities that matter most. Explore sports and physical activity, strength and conditioning, functional rehabilitation, everyday movement and personal goals.

DAY 5 — SEPTEMBER 5

CHALLENGE

PHYSIOFi Recovery Challenge

Can You Move? This online movement and engagement activity encourages people to participate safely, stay active and share their recovery journey through a guided challenge experience.

DAY 6 — SEPTEMBER 6

CONNECT

PHYSIOFi MOVE & RECOVER CAMP

Physiotherapy Beyond the Screen. After five days of online engagement, PHYSIOFi brings the campaign into the community with an interactive day focused on movement, rehabilitation and awareness.

The PHYSIOFi MOVE & RECOVER CAMP is the campaign’s primary offline event, bringing physiotherapy professionals and the community together to learn, move and reconnect.

DAY 7 — SEPTEMBER 7

CELEBRATE

Behind Every Recovery

Physiotherapy is about more than treatment. It is about helping people regain movement, confidence, independence and quality of life. Day 7 celebrates the physiotherapists, patients, athletes, healthcare professionals and communities that make recovery possible.

DAY 8 — SEPTEMBER 8

INSPIRE

World Physiotherapy Day Grand Finale

The final day brings PHYSIOFi Physiotherapy Week to its conclusion with a special online celebration for World Physiotherapy Day, including the PHYSIOFi Digital Physiotherapy Summit.

  • Sports Rehabilitation Session
