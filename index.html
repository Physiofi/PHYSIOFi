Services

Hover a box to view condition details and care focus.

Musculoskeletal

Back and neck pain, slipped discs, osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and chronic muscle strains.

Neurological

Stroke rehabilitation, Parkinson's disease, Multiple Sclerosis, Spinal Cord Injury, and balance/coordination disorders.

Mental Health

Therapeutic exercises for stress reduction, physical activity programs for depression/anxiety, and relaxation techniques.

Pre-Surgical

Conditioning and strengthening prior to elective surgeries to improve recovery outcomes (e.g., pre-ACL or pre-hip replacement).

Post-Surgical

Targeted rehabilitation following orthopedic, abdominal, or cardiac surgeries to restore mobility and reduce scar tissue.

Elderly Care

Fall prevention, mobility assistance, osteoporosis management, and age-related functional decline.

Cardio-Respiratory

Post-COPD management, asthma physical therapy, post-heart attack recovery, and improving lung capacity.

Antenatal Care

Pregnancy-related back pain, pelvic floor strengthening, and postural correction during pregnancy.

Post-natal Care

Diastasis recti recovery, pelvic floor rehabilitation, and safe return-to-exercise programs after childbirth.

Joint Replacement

Specialized protocols for Total Hip Replacement (THR), Total Knee Replacement (TKR), and Shoulder Arthroplasty.

Women's Health

Pelvic organ prolapse, incontinence, pelvic pain, and menopause-related physical changes.

Sports Injuries

Ligament tears (ACL/MCL), rotator cuff injuries, shin splints, tennis elbow, and athletic performance optimization.

PHYSIOFi EDU HUB - An Educational Initiative by PHYSIOFi

PHYSIOFi EDU HUB is the dedicated educational wing of PHYSIOFi, focused on enhancing the knowledge, skills, and career growth of physiotherapy students and professionals.

What PHYSIOFi EDU HUB Offers

It offers a range of learning opportunities including webinars, workshops, and structured training programs that bridge the gap between theoretical learning and real-world clinical practice.
